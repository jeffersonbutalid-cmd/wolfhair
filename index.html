What we do

Every proven way to bring your hair back, under one roof.

We match the method to you, your hair, and your goals. Here is what that can include.

FUE transplant

Follicular Unit Extraction moves individual grafts one at a time, leaving no linear scar and a fast, comfortable recovery.

No linear scar

FUT transplant

Follicular Unit Transplantation lifts a strip of donor tissue, ideal when you want maximum grafts in a single session.

Maximum grafts

ARTAS robotic

FDA-cleared robotic precision assists the surgeon in selecting and harvesting grafts with consistency you can see.

FDA-cleared

PRP therapy

Platelet-rich plasma uses your own blood to support existing follicles, often paired with a transplant for fuller results.

Non-surgical

Keralase

Laser energy paired with a topical hair-growth serum to refresh the scalp and encourage thicker, healthier hair.

In-office
