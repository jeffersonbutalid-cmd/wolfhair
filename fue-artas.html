What is FUE?

FUE moves your own hair, follicle by follicle.

FUE, or follicular unit extraction, removes individual follicles one at a time from a donor area where hair grows densely, then places them where hair is thinning. Because no strip of scalp is removed, FUE leaves no linear scar, recovery is quicker, and you can usually wear your hair short afterward.

The transplanted follicles come from areas that are resistant to the hormone behind most hair loss, which is why transplanted hair typically continues to grow naturally.

  • No linear scar, small dot marks that heal
  • Minimally invasive, outpatient

What is ARTAS?

Robotic precision, guided by your surgeon.

ARTAS is an FDA-cleared robotic system that assists with FUE by harvesting follicles with consistent precision. Your surgeon still plans the hairline and directs the procedure. The robot supports accurate, repeatable harvesting, which can help protect the donor area and the quality of each graft.

Wolf Hair offers FUE, ARTAS robotic FUE, and FUT, and matches the method to you rather than fitting you to one technique.

FUE and ARTAS, answered.

FUE, or follicular unit extraction, is a hair transplant method that removes individual follicles one at a time from a donor area and places them where hair is thinning. Because no strip of scalp is removed, FUE leaves no linear scar, recovery is quicker, and you can usually wear your hair short.

ARTAS is an FDA-cleared robotic system that assists the surgeon during FUE by harvesting follicles with consistent precision. The physician plans and directs the procedure, and the robot supports accurate, repeatable harvesting. Wolf Hair offers ARTAS robotic FUE in Cincinnati.

FUE removes follicles individually and leaves no linear scar. FUT removes a thin strip of scalp and can move a large number of grafts in one session, leaving a fine linear scar that is hidden by surrounding hair. The right choice depends on your goals, your donor area, and how you wear your hair.

FUE does not leave a linear scar because follicles are taken individually rather than as a strip. Tiny dot marks in the donor area heal and are typically not visible, even with short hair.

Most patients go home the same day with no overnight stay and return to normal activity within a few days. Transplanted hair sheds in the first weeks, new growth begins within a few months, and results fill in over the following year. Individual results vary.
