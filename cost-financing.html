What you pay for

What actually drives the price.

Hair transplant pricing is not one-size-fits-all. A handful of factors decide the number, and a free consultation turns them into an exact quote.

Number of grafts

The biggest factor. More grafts cover a larger area or add more density, which is why pricing ranges from a few thousand dollars upward.

Method

FUE, ARTAS robotic, or FUT. Your surgeon recommends the approach that fits your goals and your donor area.

Your goals

A focused hairline restoration is different from full crown coverage. Your plan is built around what matters most to you.
