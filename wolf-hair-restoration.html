What we offer

Choose from FUE, FUT, or ARTAS robotic

Most clinics offer one technique. Wolf Hair Restoration offers a full range, and your surgeon recommends the right fit at your free consultation.

FUE

Follicular Unit Extraction removes individual grafts with no linear scar. A good fit if you wear your hair short.

No linear scar

FUT

Follicular Unit Transplantation moves more grafts per session, suited to more advanced hair loss.

Maximum grafts

ARTAS robotic

AI-guided follicle selection assists the surgeon with precise, consistent harvesting. ARTAS is FDA-cleared.

AI-guided, FDA-cleared

Not ready for surgery? We also offer non-surgical options, including Keralase laser therapy, for earlier-stage thinning. Both men and women are treated. Individual results vary.

Before anything else

Are you a candidate? We assess three things.

No clinic should recommend surgery without examining your scalp first. Every consultation starts with the same honest, three-part assessment.

Hair loss pattern

Your type and stage of loss, and what is likely over the next 10 to 20 years, so your plan still looks right as you age.

Donor area

How much healthy density is on the back and sides. Avoiding over-harvesting starts with a proper assessment first.

Goals and lifestyle

Whether a surgical result or a non-surgical plan fits what you want. Both are valid paths, and we say so straight.

Consultation required to assess candidacy. Individual results vary.
